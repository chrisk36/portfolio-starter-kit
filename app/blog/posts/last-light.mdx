# Last Light

**Last Light** is a first-person UE5 project built around a dynamic global lighting system.  
The Sun cycles through different emissive color states that drive enemy behavior, environmental effects, movement constraints, and UI feedback.

**Controls:**  
- **WASD** to move  
- **Hold Left Mouse** to sprint  
- Mouse to look

## Core Mechanics

### Dynamic Sun System
The Sun actor uses a SunState enum and a global event dispatcher.  
Each color triggers a different gameplay state:

#### Green — Exploration
- Safe state  
- Stamina regenerates  
- Eye Monster dormant  

#### Yellow — Warning
- Eye Monster begins tracking  
- Shadows shift  
- Stamina drains faster  

#### Red — Survival
- Eye Monster teleports aggressively  
- Devils spawn  
- Lighting flickers with intense audio cues  

#### Blue — Guidance
- Angels spawn  
- Hidden paths briefly revealed  


## Movement & Stamina

### Sprinting
- **Hold Left Mouse** to sprint  
- Speed increases tension in Yellow/Red states  
- Stamina drains faster in danger  
- Low stamina produces visual distortion  


## AI Systems

### Eye Monster
- Navmesh pathfinding  
- Teleportation during Red state  
- Line-of-sight detection  
- Aggression scales with Sun state  

### Devils
- Spawn during Red  
- Stationed near map nodes  

### Angels
- Touch an angel to get +1 point
- They get scared and turn invisible if you get hurt by a demon, but you can still follow their glow to catch them

## Environment & Visual Design

### Dynamic Materials
- Emissive and Fresnel effects react to Sun color  

### Niagara Effects
- Pulse, fog, and flicker variations  
- Red state intensifies effects  

### Lighting
- Fully event-driven  
- No tick-based polling  

## UI & Player Feedback

### HUD
- Stamina bar  
- Sun state indicator  
- Warning icons  

### Menus
- Main menu (Play / Instructions / Exit)  
- End screen on defeat or escape  

## Future Improvements
