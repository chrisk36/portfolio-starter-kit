# Atelic — Landing Page Redesign

Atelic is a travel-itinerary platform that helps users plan destinations, manage commutes, and discover popular locations for smoother trip planning. This project was a client engagement through **Penn SPARK**, where our team partnered directly with Atelic to redesign and rebuild their public landing page.

## Role

**Web Redesign Team Lead** · Jan 2026 – May 2026
Atelic Travel Agency × Penn SPARK Client Team · Philadelphia, PA

I led a 10-person cross-functional team of designers and engineers through the end-to-end redesign of a client-facing travel platform, managing five product milestones across weekly Agile sprints and delivering ahead of schedule. As the sole client-facing technical lead, I ran weekly stakeholder meetings and translated 20+ business requirements into engineering specifications that guided frontend development and improved usability and brand consistency.

## The Goal

Atelic wanted a landing page that was modern and visually compelling, instantly understandable, and simple to use — replacing a page that didn't clearly communicate what the product did or reduce the "logistics anxiety" of trip planning.
