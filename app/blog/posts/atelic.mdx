# Atelic — Landing Page Redesign

Atelic is a travel-itinerary platform that helps users plan destinations, manage commutes, and discover locations for smoother trip planning. This was a client engagement through **Penn SPARK** where our team partnered directly with Atelic to redesign and rebuild their public landing page from the ground up.

## Role

**Web Redesign Team Lead** · Jan 2026 – May 2026  
Atelic Travel Agency × Penn SPARK · Philadelphia, PA

I led a 10-person cross-functional team of designers and engineers, managing **5 product milestones** across weekly Agile sprints and delivering the project ahead of schedule. As the **sole client-facing technical lead**, I ran weekly stakeholder meetings with Atelic and translated **20+ business requirements** into engineering specs that guided frontend development and improved both usability and brand consistency.

---

## 1. Hero Section

Replaced a plain, text-heavy hero with a bold illustrated section that communicates Atelic's value proposition at a glance and leads directly into a download call to action.

---

## 2. Navigation

Simplified the nav into a cleaner, higher-contrast layout — keeping key links (About, Terms, Contact) accessible without crowding the logo or creating visual noise.

---

## 3. Explanation Section

Reworked the "About Atelic" block into a mobile-friendly step-by-step walkthrough of what the app actually does, replacing a vague description that left users unclear on the product.

---

## 4. Footer

Consolidated a cluttered footer into a minimal, consistent sign-off that matches the visual tone of the redesigned page.
