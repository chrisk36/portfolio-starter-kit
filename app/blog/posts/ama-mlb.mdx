# Major League Baseball (AMA) – Marketing Campaign

Developed for the **AMA International Collegiate Case Competition (2026)** through **Penn Marketing Undergraduate Student Establishment (PUSE)**, where I served as **Vice President** leading a 7-person consulting team. Our MLB campaign placed **3rd among 90+ competing teams** internationally.

The challenge: design a research-backed marketing strategy to grow Gen Z fandom for Major League Baseball — a brand with enormous legacy but a measurable engagement gap with audiences under 25.

## My Role

As VP and team lead, I ran weekly team meetings, reviewed and critiqued each member's deliverables before submission, and mentored newer members through the research synthesis and strategy development process. I also co-planned team bonding events to keep the group aligned and energized across a multi-month competition cycle.

On the strategy side, I co-led primary research design and translated findings into the ERA framework — shaping how the campaign was structured and how each sub-campaign justified its ROI.

---

## Research

We built the strategy on primary research rather than relying on industry reports alone:

- **131 survey respondents** across Gen Z (ages 18–26) covering fandom habits, media consumption, and barriers to engagement
- **24 in-depth interviews** to understand emotional associations with baseball and what would actually move the needle

Key finding: Gen Z doesn't dislike baseball — they're largely indifferent to it. The opportunity is creating low-barrier entry points through cultural contexts they already care about, not converting skeptics.

The campaign was projected to increase Gen Z engagement by **60%**.

---

## 1. Experience — Taste of the Ballpark Tour

Bring the ballpark atmosphere to Gen Z before asking them to show up to a game:

- **Mascot Day Tailgates** — stadium-scale food and fan activations off-venue
- **Dog Derby Classic** — a beloved in-game moment scaled into a standalone cultural event
- **College Campus Promotions** — pop-up MLB experiences during high-traffic social seasons

---

## 2. Reach — MLB in Pop Culture

Embed MLB into media Gen Z already consumes daily:

- **#MajorLook Fashion Activations** — athlete-as-style-icon content through social and editorial channels
- **Latin Artist Music Partnership** — co-created content between musicians and players, tapping the crossover between Latin music, baseball's fanbase, and Gen Z streaming
- **Poppi × MLB World Series** — partnership with a Gen Z-native beverage brand tied to the sport's biggest moment

---

## 3. Align — Bridging Old and New

Connect the sport's history to the values and content habits of younger audiences:

- **Animated Short Films** — social-native animated storytelling about players and franchise history in a format Gen Z watches
- **Veteran/Rookie Series** — documentary content pairing legends with current rising stars, creating the inter-generational tension that drives fan conversation
- **Youth Programs** — access-focused investment framing baseball as a community sport
