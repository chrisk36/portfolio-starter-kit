# Adobe Express – Product Redesign

Developed for the **AMA International Collegiate Case Competition (2025)** through **Penn Marketing Undergraduate Student Establishment (MUSE)** as part of a 7-person consulting team. Our Adobe Express strategy placed **3rd among 90+ competing teams** internationally and was projected to increase user engagement by **40%**.

The challenge was to identify where Adobe Express was losing student creators and collaborative teams, then design product improvements that addressed those friction points with clear business rationale.

Rather than proposing a single fix, I explored **three distinct product directions**, each targeting a different gap in the Express workflow — from real-time collaboration to mobile-first usage and data visualization.

---

## 1. Team Hub – Centralized Collaboration

This direction introduces a **Team Hub** to centralize coordination for student organizations and group projects. Tasks, progress, and communication are surfaced in one control center, reducing the context-switching that currently pushes teams toward external tools like Notion or Slack.

The goal was to support real student workflows — clubs, class projects, creative societies — where Express is often abandoned mid-project because collaboration lives somewhere else.

---

## 2. Mobile Upload – Pinned Sharing Workflow

User research showed that mobile Express is used almost exclusively for **quick uploads and sharing**, not full design work.

I redesigned the mobile experience to **pin the Upload Content flow** at the top of the interface — making the most common action immediate rather than buried. Advanced tools stay accessible but don't compete for attention on a smaller screen.

---

## 3. Data Visualization – Chart Creation Improvements

Express's chart tools had real gaps: poor discoverability, limited CSV support, and fragmented controls that slowed down non-technical users trying to build polished visuals.

I proposed a streamlined chart workflow integrating cleaner chart-specific tooling, better data input handling, and AI-assisted generation to reduce manual setup — positioning Express to compete with standalone tools like Flourish or Datawrapper for student use cases.
