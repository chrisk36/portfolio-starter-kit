# Adobe Express – Product Redesign

This project was created for the **AMA International Collegiate Case Competition (2025)**, where our team placed **3rd internationally**. The challenge focused on identifying opportunities for **Adobe Express** to better support student creators and collaborative teams.

Rather than redesigning a single feature, I explored **multiple product directions**, each targeting a different friction point in the Express workflow—ranging from collaboration to mobile usage and data visualization.

---

## 1. Team Hub – Centralized Collaboration

This exploration introduces a **Team Hub** designed to centralize collaboration for student organizations and group projects. Tasks, progress, and team communication are surfaced in a single control center, reducing context switching and keeping collaboration embedded directly within the creative environment.

The goal was to support real student workflows—clubs, societies, and project teams—where coordination often happens across disconnected tools.

---

## 2. Mobile Upload – Pinned Sharing Workflow

User feedback showed that the mobile version of Adobe Express was primarily used for **quick uploads and sharing**, not in-depth design work.

In response, I redesigned the mobile experience to **pin the Upload Content workflow** at the top of the interface, making the most common action immediately accessible. This change prioritizes speed and clarity while keeping advanced tools available without overwhelming the smaller screen.

---

## 3. Data Visualization – Chart Creation Improvements

This exploration focused on improving **chart creation and data visualization** within Adobe Express.

I identified issues around discoverability, CSV compatibility, and fragmented controls, then proposed a streamlined chart workflow that integrates:
- clearer chart-specific tooling
- better data input handling
- AI-assisted chart generation to reduce manual setup

The intent was to make Express competitive with standalone data visualization tools while remaining accessible to non-technical users.
