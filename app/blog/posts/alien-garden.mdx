# Alien Garden

Alien Garden is a 3D environment study built around an intentionally **unsettling,
otherworldly** mood. I explored plant-like forms that feel almost familiar, but
slightly “wrong” — using exaggerated silhouettes, synthetic color accents, and
high-contrast lighting to create visual tension.

The goal was to make the scene read as a *garden* at first glance, then feel
increasingly uncanny as you notice irregular shapes, strange textures, and
unnatural color relationships.

## Design Goals

- **Uncanny organic forms:** plants that suggest biology without feeling natural
- **Color tension:** saturated accents against darker, muted base tones
- **Mood-first lighting:** lighting chosen to support unease over realism
- **Readable silhouettes:** shapes that remain distinct even in low light

## What I Focused On

### Shape Language
I leaned into stretched stems, clustered bulbs, and asymmetrical growth patterns
to keep the scene visually “alive,” but not comforting.

### Materials & Surface Detail
Reflective highlights and subtle specularity were used to make surfaces feel
slightly synthetic — as if the plants are part-organic, part-manufactured.

### Composition
I framed shots to guide attention through the scene while leaving areas of
darkness and ambiguity, reinforcing the unsettling tone.
