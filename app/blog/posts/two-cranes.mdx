# Two Cranes

My first ever project in Maya! Two Cranes is a short 3D design project created in Maya, presented as a
**360-degree rotating animation**. The piece features a stylized bird sculpture
rendered in gold, placed against a soft, cloudy background to emphasize form,
surface quality, and silhouette.

The animation focuses on clarity and restraint—allowing the viewer to observe
the sculpture from all angles without distraction. The slow rotation highlights
proportions, balance, and material response to light rather than narrative or
motion complexity.

## Design Focus

- Sculptural form and symmetry
- Material choice and reflective properties
- Clean presentation with minimal visual noise
- Use of rotation to communicate spatial structure

## Tools

- Autodesk Maya
- Polygonal modeling
- Basic lighting and material setup
- Turntable-style animation for presentation
