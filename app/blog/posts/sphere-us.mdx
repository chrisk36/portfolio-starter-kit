# SphereUs — Influencer Overlap Analysis

SphereUs is an influencer research and analytics platform that helps brands and agencies optimize influencer marketing by understanding how creators’ audiences overlap and how to balance incremental reach vs. frequency.

## Context

**Program:** Penn Spark x SphereUs (Fall 2025 Client Project) 
**My role:** Full-Stack Developer

## The Problem

Influencer campaigns don’t work as a single-touch funnel — brands often need multiple touches, but they don’t have a clear way to understand whether multiple creators are reaching new people or repeatedly hitting the same audience.

In particular, teams can usually tell which creators are popular, but not how their audiences overlap or whether a set of creators will be redundant — which turns campaign planning into guesswork.

## The Solution

SphereUs supports overlap analysis so teams can:
- view overlap between creators
- identify redundant audiences to avoid waste
- balance incremental reach and conversion-driving frequency
- track audience growth and campaign impact

## What We Built / What I Contributed

On this project, we focused on making overlap analysis *instantly understandable* and *actionable* for marketers:
- A UI that clearly communicates the purpose + value of overlap analysis
- A workflow grounded in real marketer goals and planning behavior
- A dashboard layout that prioritizes “what matters first” and reduces cognitive load

## Workflow Redesign

The original flow made it hard to understand what the overlap percentage meant and what action to take next.

### New workflow
In the redesigned flow:
- users input campaign goals (reach vs. conversion, etc.)
- users select creators to compare
- SphereUs generates overlap percentage **plus** context + action items
- the key focus is giving meaning to the overlap number (not just showing it)
