# Soft Robotics Research Engineer
**National University of Singapore** · May – Jul 2025 · Singapore

## Overview

I worked as a research engineer on soft robotic systems that convert optical stimulation into controlled mechanical motion. The project integrated light-responsive hydrogel substrates with electronic hardware, targeting repeatable real-time actuation with biodegradable materials — a combination with applications in bio-inspired design and environmentally conscious robotics.

Due to ongoing publication and institutional restrictions, detailed experimental data, fabrication parameters, and visual documentation cannot be publicly shared.

## What I Built

**Light-responsive robotic platform** — Designed and validated a system that converts optical stimulation directly into controlled mechanical motion by interfacing a custom electronic hardware layer with hydrogel substrates. Demonstrated repeatable actuation in real-time across multiple test cycles.

**Biodegradable luminescent hydrogel** — Developed and iterated on a light-emitting hydrogel achieving **50% higher luminescence** and **40% greater mechanical stability** compared to the baseline formulation, through a cycle of material optimization, fabrication, and experimental performance testing.

## Research Context

The work sat at the intersection of robotics, materials science, and applied engineering — an interdisciplinary setup where the gel's optical and mechanical properties had to be co-optimized with the hardware that actuates it. Most of the iteration happened at the fabrication level: tuning formulation parameters, testing under varied stimulation conditions, and validating that improvements held across repeated trials.
