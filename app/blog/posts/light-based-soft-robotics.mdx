# Soft Robotics Researcher  
**National University of Singapore**  

## Overview

I worked as a researcher on soft robotic systems that interface with biodegradable, light-emitting materials. The project explored how light-responsive actuation and material properties can be integrated into soft robotic platforms, with applications in responsive systems and bio-inspired design.

Due to ongoing publication and institutional restrictions, detailed technical specifications, experimental data, and visual documentation cannot be publicly shared at this time.

## Key Contributions

- Programmed a **light-responsive robotic system** designed to interface directly with a biodegradable gel, demonstrating functional integration between hardware and material behavior  
- Engineered a **biodegradable, light-emitting gel** achieving approximately **50% higher luminescence** and **40% greater stability** compared to baseline formulations  
- Collaborated within an interdisciplinary research environment combining **robotics, materials science, and applied engineering**
