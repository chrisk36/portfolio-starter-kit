# Roomies

Roomies is a mobile app designed to make coordinating life with roommates simpler — and more engaging. Instead of relying on scattered messages or passive reminders, Roomies introduces structure and lighthearted competition to everyday tasks like chores, errands, and shared responsibilities.

I focused on the **gamification system** and the design and interaction of several core user flows, helping shape how users move through the app and stay motivated over time.

## Core User Flow

On the **landing page**, users are introduced to Roomies and guided toward either signing up or logging in. After authentication, users join a shared group with their roommates. From there, the app immediately brings them into the task experience — prioritizing clarity and momentum.

Once inside a group, users can move fluidly between tasks, scheduling, progress tracking, and social features without unnecessary friction.

## Tasks & Calendar

The **Tasks page** serves as the central hub of the app. Users can view upcoming and completed tasks, quickly understand what needs attention, and see who is responsible for what.

Tasks are also mirrored in the **Calendar view**, helping users visualize deadlines over time and manage responsibilities alongside their personal schedules. This dual view supports both short-term action and long-term planning.

## Gamification & Leaderboard

To make routine chores feel more engaging, we introduced a **points-based system**. Completing tasks earns points, which feed directly into a **Leaderboard** that ranks roommates within a group.

This feature encourages friendly competition while still reinforcing accountability. The leaderboard was designed to feel motivating rather than punitive — rewarding consistency and participation rather than perfection.

## Profiles & Avatar Customization

Each user has a **Profile page** where they can adjust settings and view their activity. We also designed an **Avatar creation page**, allowing users to customize a character that represents them throughout the app.

Avatars play an important role in reinforcing identity within the group and strengthening the social, game-like feel of the experience.

## Motion & Interaction Design

Subtle **animations and transitions** were added across the app to smooth navigation between pages and reinforce user actions. These motion details help the app feel responsive and polished without distracting from core functionality.
